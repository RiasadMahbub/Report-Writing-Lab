# Template — Policy Brief / Think-Tank Commentary

Use for: writing in the voice of an LSE Grantham brief, a Climate Policy
Initiative primer, an ODI insight, or a WRI research commentary.

```markdown
# [Title: names the institution/policy being examined + your angle]

**In brief:** [2–3 sentence summary of your argument — a reader should be
able to stop here and know your position.]

## The problem

[What is the current policy, framework, or practice? Describe it neutrally
and accurately first — credibility depends on getting this right before you
critique it.]

## The gap

[Where does current practice fall short? Use evidence — a statistic, a case
example, a comparison to a better practice elsewhere.]

## What this means in practice

[1–2 concrete examples showing the real-world consequence of the gap —
a country, a sector, a specific number affected.]

## Recommendation

[A specific, actionable recommendation — named to a specific actor: "the IMF
should," "national regulators should." Avoid recommendations no one specific
could act on.]
```

## Checklist before you call it done

- [ ] Opens with a clear, stated position — not buried until the end.
- [ ] Describes the current practice accurately before critiquing it.
- [ ] Every criticism is backed by a specific number or case.
- [ ] Recommendation names who should act, not just what "should happen."
- [ ] Language is accessible — a smart non-specialist could follow it.
