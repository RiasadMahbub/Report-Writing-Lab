# Template — Corporate ESG / Sustainability Report Section

Use for: writing in the voice of a World Bank Sustainability Review or IMF
Environmental Sustainability Report section.

```markdown
## [Gerund-phrase or plain-noun subhead, e.g. "Being Energy Smart"]

[1–2 sentence abstract framing sentence — why this topic matters to the
institution's mission. No numbers yet.]

**[Bolded mini-headline].** [Agent (e.g. "the Bank," "the Fund," "World Bank
facilities") + specific verb + specific action], including [2–3 concrete
sub-actions introduced by "including" or "such as"]. In fiscal [year],
[quantified result — % or $ figure, compared to a stated baseline year].

**[Bolded mini-headline].** [Repeat pattern for a second sub-topic.]

> **[Sidebar title using a proper noun/place name]**
> [3–5 sentence human-interest case study: a named office, program, or
> project. End with one quantified outcome specific to that example.]

*Data source: [GRI code or internal dataset name].*
```

## Checklist before you call it done

- [ ] No contractions anywhere.
- [ ] Every paragraph ends on a number.
- [ ] At least one "committed to / commitment to" phrase per section.
- [ ] First-person plural only ("we," "our") — never "I."
- [ ] A rule-of-three list appears at least once (values, actions, or adjectives).
- [ ] Fiscal year, not calendar year, anchors any specific claim.
- [ ] One sidebar with a named place or program.
