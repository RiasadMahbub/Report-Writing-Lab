# Style Guide — World Bank Sustainability Review

Based on: 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023 editions.

## Section anatomy (repeats every time)

1. Bolded, gerund-phrase subhead — *"Being Energy Smart," "Waste Not," "Sharing Knowledge."*
2. One–two sentence abstract framing sentence (no numbers yet).
3. A bolded mini-headline + explanatory sentence, repeated 3–5x per section.
4. A concrete number closing nearly every paragraph.
5. A boxed callout with a named place/program as a human-interest proof point.
6. A GRI-code data tag (e.g. "GRI 305") tying the paragraph to a formal indicator.

## Sentence-level pattern (4-beat rhythm)

> **Claim → Action → Example list → Quantified result**

- Sentence 1 (claim, abstract): "Water security is a top global risk in terms of development impact."
- Sentence 2 (action, agent-first): "World Bank facilities conserve water through technological upgrades and proactive maintenance."
- Sentence 3 (example list, "including/such as"): "This includes purchasing water-efficient fixtures, checking for leaks, and investing in water-efficient HVAC systems."
- Sentence 4 (quantified result): "...saving 600 gallons of water per year."

## Word-level habits

- "Committed to / commitment to" is the single most repeated connective phrase.
- Verbs: *strengthens, fosters, mainstreams, leverages, deploys, curtails, minimizes, diverts, embeds.*
- Compound modifiers stacked before nouns: "energy- and water-efficient models."
- Rule of three everywhere: values, adjectives, verbs in triads.
- No contractions. Ever.
- First-person plural throughout ("we," "our").
- Numerals always used for stats ("25 percent," "$2 billion"), paired with a baseline year.

## Structure/format

- Short paragraphs (4–5 sentences max).
- Present tense = durable commitment; past tense = specific fiscal-year action.
- Fiscal year is the temporal anchor, never calendar year.
- Every number is source-tagged with a GRI code in the margin.
- Sidebars get punchier, concrete titles with proper nouns ("Fishing for Sustainability," "Quilling in New Delhi").

## Tone

- Confident but always paired with a forward target — achievements are never presented as "done."
- Humility markers soften claims: "we recognize," "we continue to," "we aim to."
- No negative framing of the institution — shortfalls become "redoubled efforts," not failures.

## Reusable template

```
[Gerund-phrase subhead]

[Abstract framing sentence tied to institutional mission.] [Agent + specific
verb + specific action], including [2–3 concrete sub-actions]. In fiscal
[year], [quantified result with % or $ and a comparison baseline].
[Optional: proof-point sentence naming a specific office/country/program.]
```
